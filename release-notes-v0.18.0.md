First public release of MyFinance.

**Install:** on your Android phone (Android 12 or newer), download `MyFinance-0.18.0.apk` below, open it, allow "install unknown apps" if asked, and tap Install.

**What's inside**
- Quick expense entry, plus a home-screen widget
- Daily / Calendar / Monthly views, search and filters
- Reports with a category pie chart and budget progress
- Monthly budgets per category and in total, including single-month amounts
- Accounts and net worth; investments, savings and loans on their own page
- Credit cards: bills from your spending, due dates, part payments and reward points
- Backup / restore and CSV export
- Works fully offline: no internet permission, no ads, no account
