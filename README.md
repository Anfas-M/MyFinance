# MyFinance

A simple, private money tracker for Android.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**

## What it does
- Add expenses, income and transfers in a few taps, or straight from a home-screen widget
- Daily, calendar and monthly views of your transactions, with search and filters
- Reports: spending by category, comparison with the previous period, daily average
- Monthly budgets per category and in total, with "safe to spend per day"
- Accounts and net worth: cash, bank accounts, wallets and credit cards
- Credit cards: bills worked out from your spending, due dates, part payments, reward points
- Investments, savings and loans on their own page, with their own net worth
- Backup and restore to a file, and export to Excel (CSV)
- Colour themes, dark mode and list size options

## Install (Android 12 or newer)
1. On your phone, open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and tap the `.apk` file to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to **install unknown apps**.
3. Tap **Install**. Google Play Protect may say the app is unknown because it isn't from the Play
   Store - choose **Install anyway**.

## Updating
Download the newer `.apk` from Releases and install it over the app you have - your data stays.
To be safe, make a backup first: **More → Backup & export → Create backup file**.

## Privacy
- The app has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
- No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
- Backups are saved only where you choose.
